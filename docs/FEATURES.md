# FitPot: every page and feature

This is the master list. Every phase must keep all of it. If something moves,
this file says where it lives now. (Phase 5.7 navigation in brackets.)

## Me (private to you) [Locker room: My locker. Bottom of Me: barbell with Food, Training, Progress, Targets]

### Food [barbell: Food plate. Tap it again for the food plate: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, My foods, Scan]
- Daily diary by meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, with + Add per meal
- Previous / next day
- Totals vs targets: calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre (bars)
- Food search: India (INDB), USA (USDA), Packaged (Open Food Facts), Recent, My foods
- Barcode scan, portion picker (units, grams, servings)
- Create your own food, build a recipe, edit or delete them (My foods & recipes)
- Save a portion to My foods
- Remove a logged item
- Coming soon: Plate scan (Phase 6) [food safe]

### Training [barbell: Training plate. Tap it again for the dial]
- **Log**: start workout, repeat last, start a routine, history with PRs and notes, this week vs days target, total workouts, PR count
- Workout editor: strength sets (kg / lb), cardio (distance km / mi, time, count), last time and beat it, rest timer, notes, PR screen (with Hanuman ji and the gold gada, phase 5.9), edit or delete a workout
- **Muscles**: week by week sets and volume per muscle, each exercise up / same / down / new, cardio card
- **Records**: per exercise heaviest, estimated 1 rep max, most reps, chart, sessions; cardio longest, fastest pace, longest session, count, chart
- **Routines**: create, edit, delete, start; strength sets x reps, cardio minutes
- **Setup**: goal, days per week, session length, experience, rest timer, weight and distance units, equipment (checklist and your own), limitations
- Exercise picker: search, muscle filter, only my equipment, create or delete your own exercises
- Coming soon: My plan (Phase 8) [training safe in the dial]

### Progress [barbell: Progress plate]
- Log weight (kg / lb switch), date
- Latest, change over 7 / 30 / 90 days, chart with goal line, ranges, history with delete
- Progress photos in your own Google Drive: add, compare, delete

### Targets [barbell: Targets plate]
- Daily targets: calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre
- Weight unit, distance unit, goal weight
- Body measurements: waist, chest, hips, arm, thigh, neck, body fat, cm / in, history with delete
- Export my data: Workouts, Food, Weight, Measurements (CSV), Everything (JSON)

### Also on Me
- Confirm your email banner (email accounts)
- Install FitPot card

## Crew (needs a group) [Locker room: Crew. Group switcher sits with these doors]

### Today
- CHECK IN (photo or tap), one check in for all your groups, undo
- Up to 4 photos a day, + Add another photo, activity tag
- Rest day vote
- Your week or at stake now, days left, your month
- Crew list with In / Not yet / Missed / Not started, local times, rest votes
- Rule line, timezone line, offline notice

### Calendar
- Month grid: done, missed, rest, today; previous / next month
- Current streak, best streak, total
- Day detail: pot, who showed, who paid

### Wall [Scoreboard header]
- Photo tiles, one per person, flip between photos, activity tags, counter and dots
- Previous / next day, post or add another photo, delete one photo

### Board [Scoreboard header]
- Pot moved, weeks or days settled, rest days
- Balances (ranked), Settle up, Weeks, Streaks
- Month back / forward, currency note

### Group [locker room layout]
- Passcode and Invite
- Member lockers: in today light, streak, local time and timezone, joined date, left marker
- Manager's office: workout target, photo rule, penalty, pot currency (with today's rate)
- Your groups: list, switch, join or create, leave
- Timezone note, disclaimer
